About Me

Currently: completing my BSc in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at IUT and preparing for graduate roles in embedded systems, IoT, and sustainable technology.

I am an engineer, licensed community health officer, researcher, and student leader whose work sits at the intersection of technology, health, and social impact. I hold a Higher National Diploma in Community Health and Clinical Studies from Njala University, Sierra Leone, and am completing my BSc in EEE at IUT, Bangladesh — awarded a full OIC Scholarship in open international competition.

My research focuses on designing intelligent IoT systems for sustainable agriculture in Sub-Saharan Africa. I have completed engineering internships at a nuclear power station, a thermal power plant, and a leading telecommunications company. As President of the International Students Community (500+ students, 30+ nationalities), I led governance, welfare, and cross-cultural collaboration at an institutional level.
